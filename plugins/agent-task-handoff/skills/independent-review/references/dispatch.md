# Review Dispatch and Results

## Rounds and Ledger

Apply the Skill's two-round budget per delivery workstream, not repository lifetime. Only a genuinely separate future delivery starts a new budget, not a changed revision/phase/filename/chat/account. Complete configured review at planned points before final delivery; two reviews may share one owner packet.

Sol owns `review-dispatch.json` beside worker progress: review ID, purpose/stage, candidate fingerprint, reviewer ID/actual settings, material/report paths and dispatch/delivery outcomes. Reserve before dispatch; accepted or uncertain assignments hold the slot. Release only abandoned assignments proven never accepted.

Unfinished review of unchanged candidate/scope keeps ID/slot. New assignments, repeats of completed review and revised-candidate rechecks consume rounds even in the same chat. Report-delivery failure does not refund completed review; an authorized resend keeps the same ID/slot. Reconcile missing counters from existing records, not assumed zero or routine full-history reads.

Do not automatically fill round two. Record the reason for a consequential recheck. After two rounds, fix ordinary findings, run affected tests and retain exact reviewed versions/open risks. Required extra review needs explicit user authorization; budgets cannot waive required checks or justify PASS. Records are not enforced locks.

DeepSeek writes only its report; Sol owns the review ledger, not Astra's plan/state/outcome. Direct Sol owns its own records.

## Candidate and Context

Sol dispatches at the requested/planned review point without a scheduling-only owner callback. Prepare a stable self-tested candidate. Freeze reviewed source; only authorized non-overlapping work may continue. Supply exact checkout, target files/directories, objective/stage, effective requirements, baseline/diff/fingerprint, relevant raw evidence, untested scope and verification/output boundaries. Allow relevant dependency inspection within permissions, not a prescribed review method. Exclude unrelated documents, transcripts, historical plan stacks, credentials/auth files and unrelated private data.

After review recheck changed facts/candidate integrity, not stable history/baselines. Read-only instructions are not filesystem locks.

Reuse healthy context for targeted verification/unfinished review. Fresh comprehensive final context requires explicit user authorization for that serial choice; an owner plan alone is insufficient. Confirm the old reviewer ended or is isolated, with one active reviewer and unchanged budget. No new chat simply for round two or owner wakeup for scheduling/context alone.

For authorized fresh review, supply current requirements/candidate/raw evidence, not earlier conversations/approval narratives. Reconcile prior findings after independent assessment without concealing critical unresolved risks or claiming statistical independence/full blindness.

## Findings and Acceptance

Sol verifies against source/raw evidence, records accepted/rejected/unresolved findings, batches ordinary fixes and runs affected tests. Failed reproduction does not disprove a credible risk; preserve independent critical findings. Severity labels/ordinary bugs alone do not require Astra.

Astra-led work promptly escalates evidence-backed plan gaps/contradictions or requirement/design/scope/safety decisions. Stop the affected path; continue independently authorized work. Direct Sol resolves within authority or asks the user, never starts Astra.

Deliver current source/diff/fingerprint, covered/remaining scope, actual checks/raw-evidence paths, exact reviewed/rechecked versions, finding disposition, deviations and unresolved risks. Astra-led packets also follow the owner's cumulative delivery protocol; direct Sol delivers this evidence locally. Patched code is not DeepSeek-approved unless actually reviewed. Broad changes need appropriate verification, not unlimited nominal small rechecks. DeepSeek approval never replaces owner acceptance; only overall PASS with required work/checks/processes resolved permits the owner outcome.
