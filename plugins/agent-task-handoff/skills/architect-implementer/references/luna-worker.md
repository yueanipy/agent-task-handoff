# Mechanical Luna Work

Luna executes fixed-rule mechanical work of any volume with clear inputs, rules, outputs and checks needing little judgment. Tiny work may stay local; ambiguous design/debugging/integration stay with Sol/Astra. Explain an unsuitable explicit Luna choice and ask before dispatch or substitution.

Astra may assign standalone mechanical work directly to Luna without inserting Sol. Saved checkpoints, result evidence, independent owner acceptance and final outcome apply even to read-only work.

Direct or Astra-led Sol may create/reuse the workstream's one independent Luna. Use [checkpoint.md](checkpoint.md) for record ownership, [model-verification.md](model-verification.md) for actual identity/assignment and [dispatch.md](dispatch.md) for native dispatch. Give non-overlapping scope and the caller's callback identity/settings; nested Luna has `luna-progress.md`, standalone Luna main progress.

Large work may use bounded sequential batches in the same chat. Record meaningful results, failures, pending work/processes and version-bound evidence. Return the completed assigned result or a genuine specification decision, not batch/status/quota callbacks. Stop a conflicting path while continuing only independently authorized work. Actual messages use [delivery.md](delivery.md); faults use [recovery.md](recovery.md), including its optional-Luna exception.

The caller checks actual output/evidence before integration and returns concrete rework to the same healthy Luna. Only accepted mechanical scope with required checks/processes resolved permits its final outcome/completed timestamp, not completion of a larger unfinished task. No forced three-model chain.
