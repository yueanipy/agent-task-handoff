# Milestone Delivery

## Local Substeps

Sol implements, tests, repairs ordinary failures and rechecks affected behavior within the effective contract. Record changes, failures, remaining work and evidence in existing progress, not new documents/callbacks per component, test group, revision or status. Routine DeepSeek findings stay with Sol.

Sol may group substeps into coherent milestones locally without a callback or contract revision merely for grouping. Do not change scope/design/required gates. Without named milestones, use the assignment's bounded deliverable and required checks, not open-ended project work.

## Owner Milestones and Decisions

Submit a consolidated candidate at an independently verifiable deliverable or required risk/contract gate, including preceding unreviewed substeps. Complete its required work/checks first. A short assignment may have one milestone; neither defer a ready one for unrelated future work nor manufacture partial completion for a check-in.

Evidence-backed plan gaps/ambiguities/contradictions or requirement/design/scope/safety decisions needing owner judgment escalate promptly with options and the needed decision. Stop the affected path, continue independently authorized work; do not cross required acceptance gates. Ordinary implementation questions stay with Sol, not relabelled design conflicts.

Astra consolidates known findings into rework for the same healthy Sol. Sol repairs/rechecks before resubmission; new important defects may need further review. Every completed Astra-led assignment requires final verifiable delivery and independent Astra acceptance.

## Cumulative Candidate Packet

Cover the full delta since the last Astra-reviewed candidate (initial assignment baseline before first review), plus still-open findings/checks. Sending alone does not advance the reviewed baseline. Cross-check against existing diff/progress/evidence, not recent memory; pending earlier results remain included.

Lead with important changes without dropping other in-scope work, failures or limitations. Include assignment/worker identity, exact checkout, baseline/candidate fingerprints, covered/remaining scope, actual checks/evidence paths, Luna results if used, DeepSeek status/reports/disposition, deviations and unresolved risks/decisions.

Save progress and freeze relevant candidate source during review; only authorized non-overlapping work may continue. Conclusions bind to that version. Stage acceptance is not overall PASS; unrun checks remain NOT_TESTED or BLOCKED.

Messages carry compact deltas and local evidence pointers, not transcripts. [delivery.md](delivery.md) governs sends; unfinished interrupted work stays local under [recovery.md](recovery.md), not a completed milestone. No time-based reporting or submission-count quota replaces these boundaries.
